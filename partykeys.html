Chord naming

Stops the phone or tablet dimming and locking while this tab is in front, so the keyboard stays readable with both hands busy. The lock is dropped as soon as you switch away, and it costs battery while it is held. Off: the screen only stays awake while a song is actually playing.
When a song's chords are grouped by key, the app moves to whichever of those keys the chord being played belongs to — so a song that modulates takes the scale colouring and the note spelling with it. Only the keys you filled in for that song are ever used.
On: every chord is called as the nearest of the key's own seven, so an F triad over an E bass reads F/E rather than Fmaj7/E — simpler to read, at the cost of the extension. Off: the most literally accurate name, extensions and all. Note spelling follows the key either way (E♭ in flat keys, D♯ in sharp ones).
